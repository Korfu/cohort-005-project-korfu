# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy video courses, work through their lessons, and can ask for their money back shortly after buying.

## Buying and access

**Purchase**:
A learner paying for a course, recorded with the amount they actually paid.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A learner's access to a course's lessons.

**License**:
One person's place in a course, bought by a team as part of a single Purchase. A learner claims a License to get their Enrollment.
_Avoid_: Seat, coupon, licence

## Getting money back

**Refund**:
A learner asking for the money they paid for a Purchase back. A Refund is requested, then approved or declined. An approved Refund returns the money and ends the learner's Enrollment in that course.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Period**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which the learner may request a Refund.
_Avoid_: Refund window, cancel window, cooling-off period

**Chargeback**:
A payment dispute a cardholder raises through their bank. It is not a Refund, and Cadence does not handle chargebacks.
